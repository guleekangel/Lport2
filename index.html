我们慢，
是刻意的。

每一件委托都走同样的四个阶段。没有捷径，也没有图册可挑—— 如果一件东西不适合你的房间，我会直说。

Every commission runs through the same four stages. There is no fast track and no catalogue to choose from — if a piece doesn’t belong in your room, we will say so.

01

沟通Enquiry

围绕房间、光线，以及它实际会被怎么用的一次对话。需要你提供照片与平面图。

A conversation about the room, the light and how it will actually be used. We ask for photographs and a plan.

02

制图Drawing

标注尺寸的方案、材料样板，以及为定制件做的 1:1 实物打样。

Measured proposals, material samples and a full-size mock-up of any piece custom to the project.

03

制造Making

由合作多年、彼此熟悉的手艺人在合作的工坊里完成。不转包，不招标。

Made in our partner workshops by makers we have worked with for years. Nothing is subcontracted to tender.

04

安装Installation

由我自己的团队按约定日期送货与安装。一年后上门复紧、重新上油。

Delivered and installed by our own team, with a fixed date. We return a year later to tighten and re-oil.

关于我 · About Me

一位设计师，
一长串手艺人的名字，
和一种慢方法。

我是室内家具设计师。起步于一件委托、一把借来的喷枪， 和一间允许我下班后留下来的工坊。作品越来越多，方法没变： 先画出这个房间真正需要什么，再去找能做得好的人来把它做出来。

十年下来，我做过客厅里一张用了很多年的沙发，也做过整层的办公家具。 尺度在变，下的功夫没变——家具是给人用的，不是给照片看的。 大部分项目是私宅，偶尔也有会所与办公空间。

I am an interior furniture designer. I started with a single commission, a borrowed spray gun and a workshop that let me stay after hours. The work has grown since; the method has not. I draw what a room actually needs, then find the maker who can build it well.

Ten years in, I have made a sofa that a family uses daily and a full floor of office furniture. The scale changes; the method does not — furniture is for use, not for photographs. Most of my work is for private residences, with occasional hospitality and workplace projects.
